Derived from DarkRP and rewritten for s&box (C# scripts instead of Lua):
- game logic: jobs, money and salaries, police (wanted, warrants, arrests), mayor (laws, lockdown, lottery),
  doors, voting, hunger, hits, chat commands, shipments and buyable entities (scripts/darkrp/core, modules)
- the default jobs, shipments, entities, ammo, food, categories and settings (scripts/darkrp/core/RPDefaults.csx,
  RPConfig.csx), with s&box models and clothing instead of Half-Life 2 / Counter-Strike content
- the weapons: arrest/unarrest baton, stunstick, battering ram, keys, lockpick, pocket, medic kit, weapon checker,
  keypad checker and the DarkRP guns (scripts/darkrp/weapons, darkrp/weapons)
- the weapon icons in ui/darkrp/weapons (converted from DarkRP's content/materials/vgui/entities)
- disabled_defaults (darkrpmodification's disabled_defaults.lua) and a prop protection following the ideas of
  Falco's Prop Protection (FPP), which ships with DarkRP (scripts/darkrp/modules/RPPropProtection.csx)

The guns, and the arm animations of the batons and the door ram, are Sandbox's (MIT, Facepunch Studios). The stun
baton, door ram, law board and money models in models/darkrp were made for this addon.
